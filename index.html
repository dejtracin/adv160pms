    Before every ride

    Quick checks Honda recommends before each ride. Ticks clear when you reload.

      Notes from the manual

      Rain, dust and hard riding

      Riding in rain, dust, or at full throttle a lot means servicing sooner. Change the air filter more often and empty the breather drain tube more often.

      Shop jobs

      Items marked "shop job" need special tools or know-how. Honda recommends having them done at a Honda shop unless you have the tools and experience.

      Engine oil

      Use 4-stroke motorcycle oil with the grade your manual lists (Honda recommends Pro Honda 10W-30). Check the level with the scooter standing straight on flat ground, and don't overfill.

      Coolant

      With the engine cold, the level in the coolant tank should be between the upper and lower marks. Use Honda's ready-mixed coolant, and never open the radiator cap while the engine is hot.

      Brake fluid

      Use only the fluid type printed on the brake fluid cap, from a sealed bottle. A slowly dropping level usually means worn pads. A sudden drop means a leak, so get it checked right away.

      Tires

      Check pressure before riding, when tires are cold, using the numbers in your manual. Replace tires when the tread is worn down to the wear marks or is cut, cracked or uneven.

      Smart key

      When the key battery gets low, the light on the key stops blinking normally. Keep a spare battery, and learn how your manual says to start the scooter if the key battery dies.

      After a long rest or a fall

      Do the before-ride checks, make sure the battery is charged, and have the brakes, steering and shocks looked at before riding again.
